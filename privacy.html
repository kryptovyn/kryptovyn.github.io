Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-06

This policy covers the KRYPTOVYN website (kryptovyn.github.io), KRYPTOVYN's web games, and the "KRYPTOVYN Video Poster" tool.

Games and website

The games save progress (gold, upgrades, settings) only in your own browser. KRYPTOVYN does not collect your name, email or other personal data. Game portals such as Playgama may show ads and collect data under their own privacy policies.

KRYPTOVYN Video Poster (TikTok)

The tool is used only by KRYPTOVYN with KRYPTOVYN's own TikTok account. When KRYPTOVYN signs in with TikTok, the tool receives basic account information (user.info.basic) and permission to upload and post videos (video.upload, video.publish). The access token is stored only on KRYPTOVYN's own computer and is used only to post KRYPTOVYN's gameplay videos. No data from other TikTok users is collected, stored, sold or shared.

Deleting data

KRYPTOVYN can revoke the tool's access at any time in TikTok's settings, which ends its access.
